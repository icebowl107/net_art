Tigers and a Strawberry

A man traveling across a field encountered a tiger.
He fled, the tiger after him. Coming to a precipice,
he caught hold of the root of a wild vine and swung
himself down over the edge.

  1. The tiger sniffed at him from above
  2. Trembling, the man looked down
  3. another tiger was waiting to eat him
Only the vine sustained him.
Two mice,
  • one white,
  • one black,
little    by    little    started to gnaw away at
the vine. The man saw a luscious strawberry near him.
Grasping the vine with one hand, he plucked the
strawberry with the other. How sweet it tasted!
