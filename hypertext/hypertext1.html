You wake up. Nothing is familiar.

You need to leave. You need to leave. You need to leave.

The smell of old wood fills the air, slightly dizzying you as you take deep breaths.
Your eyes adjust to the dim light before finally focusing on the three doors in front of you.
You find yourself sitting down, with your back against a wall. The room is warm. Quite comforting actually.
Yet something tells you that you are not welcome here. It would be best for you to leave.

You need to leave. You need to leave. You need to leave.

Your attention turns towards the doors again. The doors compell you.
You get up. And you enter...

  • The left door.
  • The middle door.
  • The right door.
